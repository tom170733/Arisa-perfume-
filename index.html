About Us

Our Story

Welcome to ARISA PERFUME STORE, the premier destination for high-end fragrances in Freetown, Sierra Leone. Founded on a passion for exquisite scents, we believe that a perfume is more than just a signature—it is a reflection of your personality.


We carefully curate our collection from the finest fragrance houses across the globe to ensure that every bottle represents true luxury. Whether you are looking for something bold and adventurous or soft and sophisticated, we are here to help you find your signature match.

Store Policies

Delivery & Shipping

We offer fast and reliable delivery within 24 hours to any location in Freetown. For customers outside the capital, please contact us via WhatsApp for special arrangements.


Authenticity Guarantee

At ARISA, we guarantee that all our perfumes are 100% authentic. Quality and trust are our top priorities; we do not stock imitations.


Payment Options

For your convenience, we accept Cash on Delivery, Orange Money, and Afrimoney. Please confirm your payment method during checkout.


Returns

Due to health and safety regulations regarding fragrance products, all sales are final. We encourage customers to inspect their package thoroughly upon delivery.
